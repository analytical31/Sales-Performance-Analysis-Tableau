# Sales Performance Dashboard (Tableau)</br>
An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes sales, profit, and order trends across product categories, regions, customer segments, and shipping modes.</br>
## Overview</br>
This dashboard gives a quick view of business performance. It tracks key KPIs and lets users explore where revenue and profit come from, and which products and customer groups drive them.</br>

## Dashboard Components</br>
#### KPI Summary:</br>
Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders and overall Profit Ratio at the top of the dashboard.</br>
#### Trend Analysis:</br>
Monthly line chart of the selected metric (2024–2026) by category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology). It shows seasonality and growth patterns, with Technology leading in most months.</br>
#### Sales and Profit Map:</br>
Geographic view of performance by state, with profit shown as color intensity. Top-performing states include California, Texas, New York and Washington.</br>
#### Profit Ratio by Sub-category:</br>
Ranked bar chart comparing sub-categories such as Phones, Laptops, Desks, Chairs, Monitors and Accessories, to spot the most and least profitable products.</br>
#### Segment by Ship Mode:</br>
Stacked bar chart showing how Consumer, Corporate and Home Office customers split across shipping modes (First Class, Same Day, Second Class, Standard Class).</br>
## Key Insights</br>
Technology is the top-performing category and shows the highest peaks in the trend.</br>
Phones and Laptops lead in the sub-category ranking.</br>
The Consumer segment contributes the most, and Standard Class is its most-used shipping mode.</br>
Sales and profit are concentrated in a few states, mainly on the West Coast, Texas and the Northeast.</br>
## Features</br>
Dynamic metric selection (Sales / Profit) through a parameter</br>
Color-coded legends for Category, Profit, Profit Ratio and Ship Mode</br>
Interactive filtering across all charts</br>
